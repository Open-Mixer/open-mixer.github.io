Refusal and undo-label codes

Nothing below the surface ever emits display prose. A refusal, a warning or an undo-entry label travels as a stable code plus a small record of named parameters, and the client renders it in the operator's own locale. 229 codes, generated from the registry that also gates the server's own build.
