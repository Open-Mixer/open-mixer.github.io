Zynthian: interim figures

What a Raspberry Pi 5 with an RME audio interface has measured so far, published before its matrix is complete. These figures are preliminary: the reference-tier matrix replaces this page at the next release.

01

What these figures are

  • Hardware tier: Raspberry Pi 5 Model B Rev 1.0, 4 cores, 4049 MB, with an RME Babyface Pro over USB.
  • Rates covered: 48 kHz, 96 kHz. Quanta covered: 128, 256 frames.
  • 192 kHz was not measured in these passes; the tables by period length below have it.
  • Bench: 1248 cells measured at the rates shown; 1132 ran, 116 failed and stay listed, and the stability gate marked 51 of the 1132 as unstable.
  • Qualification grid: 0 of 22 cells finished; the grid is still running.
  • Preliminary: the reference-tier matrix replaces these figures at the next release.
  • No host is ranked against another from a partial matrix: only plugin-hostd's own figures are shown.
  • Every figure is measured digitally inside the host; nothing here is a claim about the analog path.

By period length

The matrix read by period length (plugin-hostd 0.20.3, omx-clap-host 0.31.2; JACK with the alsa driver at real-time priority 70 on the RME Babyface Pro). Rates are compared at equal period length: 512 frames at 48 kHz, 1024 at 96 kHz and 2048 at 192 kHz are the same 10.67 ms.

A cell is the frames, then clean or the xruns summed over its rows, then per host the rows with no xrun over the rows that ran. The safe minimum of a rate is its shortest clean period with every longer one clean. A period below one millisecond is no verdict on the rate.
