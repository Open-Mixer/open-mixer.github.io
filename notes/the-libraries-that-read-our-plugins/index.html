Technical note

The libraries that read our plugins

Every owner ships the reader of what it writes, in the languages it is read from. Per owner and per language, what to install, the entry point and one call, copied from the owner's repository.

Each piece of OpenMixer writes one kind of data: omx-contract the vocabulary and the scales, plugin-hostd's protocol the words of the wire, plugin-db and plugin-qualify the records and the pins, omx-panel the layouts, omx-plugins the descriptors and presets of its own plugins. Each one also ships the reader of what it writes. A program that shows a plugin, as Zynthian's interface does, reads through those readers and keeps no copy of its own.

This note lists them, per owner and per language: what to install, the entry point, and one call. Every call below is copied from the owner's repository, with the file and line it comes from. Where the repository has no example, the note says so rather than make one up.

The packages are named rpm first, then deb. Every dependency between these packages states a floor and a ceiling, the next minor below 1.0 and < 4 for omx-contract; the rule is written down once, in plugin-hostd's tests/clap_core_floor.py.

Not there yet

  • plugin-qualify cites a Python reader of record figures for Zynthian (tools/record_figures.py) that does not exist: there is no Python reader of records or of the pins file.
  • omx-contract's Python modules are not on sys.path; every program loads them by their file, as plugin-db's text.py does. A package is on its way, python3-omx-contract (import omx_contract.catalog).
  • Three kinds of name a client compares against are not constants of their owner's reader yet, so the Zynthian fork writes them as text on a few listed lines, each naming the owner's issue: which refusals are transient (plugin-hostd-protocol), the verbs and the reply codes in the Python client (plugin-hostd-protocol) and the grouping reasons of grouping.json (plugin-hostd).
  • omx-contract's /validate export, its generated omx_contract.py and plugin-qualify's readDistributedLayer have no example in their repositories.
  • omx-dsp's TypeScript twins and plugin-hostd's TypeScript client are on npm only, not in the rpm or deb packages; plugin-db's TypeScript twin is private.
